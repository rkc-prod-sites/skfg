Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Generally a 20% deposit is required, though low deposit options (10% or less) are available for eligible buyers, particularly first home buyers. We'll assess your situation and find the right path.
It can be challenging with a low credit score or limited deposit, but with the right advice and preparation, securing a mortgage is absolutely achievable. That's exactly what we're here for.
Rates vary by lender and loan type. We constantly monitor the market and have access to special rates not available directly to the public. Contact us for a personalised rate comparison.
Pre-approval typically takes 1–2 business days once we have your documents. Full approval after finding a property usually takes 3–5 business days depending on the lender.
Most lenders require home and contents insurance at minimum. We also recommend reviewing life insurance, mortgage protection, and income protection to safeguard your repayments.
Key criteria include stable income, good credit history, and a minimum deposit. You may be eligible for the First Home Grant or First Home Loan scheme. Book a free consultation and we'll assess your eligibility.
